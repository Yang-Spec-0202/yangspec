BEFORE YOU SEND

Make it fit.
Then give it a look.

A small file is useful only if the details you need are still readable.

  1. Check the receiving form

    Look up its accepted format and size limits. Maximum dimensions are different from an exact crop.

  2. Prepare an attachment

    Choose your image, or try the public example. Adjust the limits and compare the result.

  3. Open your download

    Review fine text at full size before sending. The receiving service decides whether to accept it.
