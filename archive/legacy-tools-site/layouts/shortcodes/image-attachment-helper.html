02 Set your requirements

Use the requirements from your email or upload form. The tool keeps proportions, does not crop or enlarge, and outputs at most 4096 px per side.

JPG is lossy. PNG size is reduced by resizing, with your permission.

1 KB = 1000 bytes. Blank = no size limit. Check whether your form uses KB or KiB (1024 bytes).

These are maximum dimensions, not an exact crop. Blank = original size, capped at 4096 px per side.

The tool tries the highest setting that fits, up to 92/100. This setting is not a visual quality score. PNG ignores it.

Optional: up to 8 smaller attempts, stopping at a longest side of 64 px. Small text may become unreadable. A limit may still be impossible.
