Updated October 8, 2026. YangSpec is a static website. Its tools do not require an account.

## Images stay on your device

The Image Attachment Helper reads and processes a file you choose in your browser. The tool does not upload your image, send its contents, filename or settings to analytics, put them in a URL, or save them in browser storage. The original and result are held for use on the current page.

**Try a public example** requests a sample PNG from this website. It does not request or transmit a personal file. Once loaded, the sample uses the same local processing as a file you choose.

Clear removes the page’s image references and resets its settings. Leaving the page releases its processing resources. This does not delete downloaded files or guarantee removal of temporary data managed by your browser or operating system. Review your browser’s downloads and settings when using a shared device.

## Planner inputs and clipboard

App choices, capacity inputs and monthly quotes are calculated in your browser. The planner does not submit these values to a server, add them to a share URL or save them between visits. **Copy plan** writes a summary to your clipboard only when you choose it. Keep a copied plan private if it contains information you do not wish to share.

## Theme preferences

The site can save your light or dark theme choice in browser local storage. You can remove this preference through your browser’s site-data settings. Images and planner inputs are not stored with it.

## Hosting and page analytics

Requests for pages and public assets pass through our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Hosting and security services may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses, browser details and requested URLs to deliver and protect the site. These requests are separate from local image processing and planner calculations.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page visits and loading performance. The beacon can report page paths, referrers, page-load identifiers and performance measurements. According to [Cloudflare’s RUM documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/speed/observatory/rum-beacon/), it does not use cookies or browser storage, and the network IP address is discarded after receipt at the nearest Cloudflare data center.

Our RUM setting excludes connections through Cloudflare’s listed European data centers, including the EEA/EU, Switzerland and the UK. The exclusion depends on the data center handling the connection, not your nationality. See the linked documentation for the current list and collection details.

We do not send tool files, settings, planner values or download-completion events to this service. Page views are not a count of successful tool tasks. Your browser settings or extensions can block analytics requests.

## Advertising, links and contact

The current site has no advertising or active affiliate purchase links. If that changes, we will update this policy and disclose the relationship on the relevant pages. Ordinary links to other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies; our tools do not automatically pass your files or planner inputs to them.

If you email us, we receive the information you include in that message. Please do not send private images, passwords, identity documents or payment details. For privacy questions, email **contact@yangspec.com**.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the site’s data handling changes. The update date appears at the top.
